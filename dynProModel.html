Optimal pension funding (DP oracle)

Dynamic programming over the funding ratio F = R/L and the salary ratio ρ = S/L: given the economics, what should an employer contribute each year, and how does that compare with the flat and age-related plans used in practice? Set the assumptions, then run it.

This is a model explorer, not advice. The two market plans are illustrative defaults, not sourced from Belgian plan data — change them to your own. Every simulated figure is shown with the range across five random seeds. The DP solve takes a couple of seconds.
